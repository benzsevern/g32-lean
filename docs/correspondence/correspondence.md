## Modeling conventions

**MC1 (the printed PEL family and its generic Hodge group).** The printed "effective PEL family defined by (D, T)" (G32 v0.18 lines 688–710) is Abdulali's Kuga family over Γ\D: Γ a torsion-free arithmetic subgroup of the D-linear polarization isometry group, D the corresponding period domain (Abdulali, Ann. Math. 155 (2002), §2.2 for the period domain and the family, §2.5 for the general member). This packet represents the printed family by its period domain, `PELSplitDomain` and its retained connected component `PELSplitComponent` for the reference `PELSplitReference.ofTypeIII Q` built from the member's own type III data. Its members are the domain points; its generic Hodge group is the Hodge group at a Hodge-generic point (`pel_exists_hodge_generic_point`, `pelDomainHodgeGroup`), which Lean identifies with the connected PEL isometry group (`PELTypeIIIGenerality.lean`).

Why Γ, torsion-freeness and the Kuga scheme do not affect Hodge groups: the fibre over the class of a point z ∈ D is the abelian variety whose weight-one Hodge structure is the one given by z on the fixed rational lattice, so its Hodge group is the Hodge group of z. Points in one Γ-orbit give fibres related by an element of Γ, a rational isometry commuting with D, so their Hodge groups are conjugate by that element and the generic group is a Γ-stable normal subgroup; Γ only identifies points, and torsion-freeness only makes Γ\D a manifold and the Kuga family a scheme. None changes the Hodge structure carried by a point. "The general member" (line 710) is therefore a point whose Hodge group equals the Hodge group at a Hodge-generic point, as in the `hgeneral` of the endpoint.

Scope: this identification is a modeling convention of the correspondence, not a Lean declaration. Lean does not define the arithmetic quotient family Γ\D or the Kuga scheme, and building them is out of scope for this release (decision of the author, 2026-11-14). Supporting evidence for families that are given in Lean: `pel_family_group_identification` identifies the family group of any `F : PELSplitFamily R` with the connected PEL group.
