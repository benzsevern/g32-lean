 \subsection{The construction in one page}\label{subsec:map}
 \emph{The family.} Let $G\subset GL_4(K)$ be the Shephard--Todd group $G_{32}$, of order $155\,520$, generated by the four matrices of Appendix~\ref{app:tuple}, and $\rho$ its natural representation on $K^4$, with character field $K$. Its reflections have order three, with nontrivial eigenvalue $\omega$ (class $\mathcal R_1$) or $\omega^2$ (class $\mathcal R_2$), $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$. Let $\cI$ be the class of elements with eigenvalues $(1,1,-1,-1)$. We consider Galois covers $C\to\PP^1$ with group $G$, branched over thirteen points, with local monodromy in $\mathcal R_1$ at six of them, in $\mathcal R_2$ at six, and in $\cI$ at one. Such a cover has genus $505\,441$, by Riemann--Hurwitz. By the Chevalley--Weil formula, $\rho$ occurs in $H^1(C)$ with multiplicity six and Hodge numbers $(3,3)$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:sixfold}); the multiplicity space is the first cohomology of an abelian sixfold $A_\rho(C)\subset J(C)$ of Weil type for $K$, the image of an endomorphism of $J(C)$ given by an element of $\Q[G]$. Without the point in $\cI$ the multiplicity would be four, giving fourfolds. Thirteen points give a family of dimension $13-3=10$, one more than the dimension $9$ of the moduli space.
