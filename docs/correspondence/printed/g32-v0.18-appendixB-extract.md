 918  \emph{How the data are computed.} The group is built in GAP from the four matrices of Appendix~\ref{app:tuple}, with its character table. A product-one generating tuple in the class datum is fixed. For each supplier, $H'_i$ is given by words in the generators and $\chi_i$ by its values on them; the branch data come from the double cosets $H'_i\backslash G/\langle g_j\rangle$, and $a(\chi_i)$ from the restrictions $\tau|_{H'_i}$ by Frobenius reciprocity. The words are not in the matrices of Appendix~\ref{app:tuple}: they are words in the four generators $f_1,\dots,f_4$ used by the certificate (\texttt{certificate/A/verify\_fixed\_witness.g}, printed in Section~0 of its output), which are reflections in $\mathcal R_1$ of the certificate library's realisation of $G_{32}$, related to the generators of Appendix~\ref{app:tuple} by an explicit isomorphism (\texttt{certificate/A/f\_to\_appA.json}, which writes each $f_i$ as a word in $r_1,r_2,x$); a reader evaluating the words must use those generators. The checking script reads these fixed data, recomputes every row, and checks the identity without reference to the order of the characters: for every non-real $\tau$ with $m_\tau>0$ it computes $\sum_in_i(\langle\tau|_{H'_i},\chi_i\rangle-\langle\bar\tau|_{H'_i},\chi_i\rangle)$ and requires $+1$ at $\tau=\rho$, $-1$ at $\tau=\bar\rho$ and $0$ otherwise, and for every quaternionic $\tau$ with $m_\tau>0$ it requires $\sum_in_i\langle\tau|_{H'_i},\chi_i\rangle$ to be even. A second script, not using GAP, rechecks the identity and the simplicity of the exponents from the exported data. The suppliers were found among the linear characters, of orders $3$ to $18$, of representatives of the conjugacy classes of subgroups of $G$.
